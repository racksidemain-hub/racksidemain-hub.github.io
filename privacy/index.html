Privacy policy

Developer: Jerome Ortega, Singapore · Effective 18 September 2026

Rackside is a notebook for field technicians on iPad. In short: we don't collect any of your data. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPad unless you choose to export it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer never receives your notes, photos, location or any other information.

What stays on your iPad

These are stored only in the app on your iPad:

  • Your entries: site surveys, troubleshooting logs, discussions, brainstorms, sketches and notes
  • Photos you take or add, and any markup you draw on them. Location information is removed from photos.
  • GPS coordinates you save to a site survey
  • Client signatures, with the signer's name, title and signing time
  • Your profile (name, title, company), templates, equipment presets and settings

Subscriptions

Rackside Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the purchase, your payment method and any refunds under Apple's privacy policy; Rackside never sees your card details or Apple Account.

To know whether you're subscribed, the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPad. It receives a signed record of your subscription (the product, and its start and end dates) and nothing else about you. The developer doesn't receive it and there is no server of ours involved.

Permissions

Each permission is optional. iPadOS asks the first time you use the feature, and you can change it any time in the iPad's Settings app › Rackside. The rest of the app works without them.

  • Camera: to take photos to attach to site surveys and troubleshooting logs.
  • Location (while using the app): to save the iPad's position to a site survey, only when you tap Use Current Location. There's no address lookup.
  • Notifications: reminders about due action items, if you turn reminders on, and one reminder two days before a free trial ends. Both are scheduled on the iPad itself.

Adding a photo from your library uses the iPadOS photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose.

Exports and backups

You can export PDF reports and backup files. These are files you create and choose where to send, such as Files, AirDrop, Mail or another app. Once shared, they're handled by that app or service and whoever you send them to, not by us.

Backup files are not encrypted. Anyone who has a backup file can read its contents, so keep it somewhere you trust.

If you tap a link in an entry or open a saved location in Maps, it opens in Safari or Apple Maps, which have their own privacy policies.

iPad backups

If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, iPadOS may include this app's data in that backup. That is controlled by your iPad's settings and Apple's services, not by the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries in the app at any time. Deleting the app from your iPad deletes all of its data. Copies you exported, and your iPad's own backups, aren't affected.

Children

The app is made for work use and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
