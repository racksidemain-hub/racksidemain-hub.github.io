Subscription terms

  • Rackside Pro — Yearly is a 1-year auto-renewing subscription, and Rackside Pro — Monthly is a 1-month auto-renewing subscription. The price of each is shown in the App Store and in the app before you buy.
  • New subscribers get a 14-day free trial, once per Apple Account. When the trial ends, the plan you chose starts and is charged to your Apple Account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period (a year or a month) unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel in Rackside › Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription, or in the iPad's Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Without an active trial or subscription, Rackside is read-only: you can still open, search, export and back up everything you've created.
