Rackside support

Rackside is a field notebook for network, AV, IT and low-voltage technicians on iPad. Survey the site, log the fault, sketch the fix and hand over a PDF report. Everything stays on your iPad.

Need help? Email rackside.sup@outlook.com. Tell us your iPad model, the iPadOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Subscription

How much does Rackside cost?

Rackside is free to download. Rackside Pro — Yearly is a 1-year auto-renewing subscription. In Singapore it's S$25.98 a year; the App Store shows the price in your own currency before you buy. New subscribers get 7 days free.

How does the free trial work?

Tap Start 7-Day Free Trial and confirm with Apple. Apple asks for a payment method on your Apple Account, but nothing is charged during the 7 days. Rackside reminds you two days before the trial ends. If you don't cancel, the yearly subscription starts when the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

In Rackside, open Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription. You can also go to the iPad's Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Rackside. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current year to avoid the next charge. You keep full access until then.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Rackside switches to read-only mode. You can still open and search every entry, export PDF reports and back up everything. Creating new entries and editing needs a subscription. Nothing is ever deleted or hidden.

I got a new iPad. How do I get my subscription back?

Sign in to the same Apple Account, open Rackside, then Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPad. Rackside has no accounts and no cloud, and it doesn't send your notes, photos, locations or signatures anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up, or move to a new iPad?

Open Settings › Data › Export Backup and save the file somewhere safe, such as Files, a computer or AirDrop to your new iPad. On the new iPad, open the backup file or use Settings › Data › Restore from Backup. Restoring adds what's missing and never overwrites or deletes anything.

Backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Does deleting the app delete my notes?

Yes. Deleting Rackside deletes everything stored in it. Export a backup first if you might want your notes later.

Using Rackside

Which devices does Rackside run on?

iPad with iPadOS 26 or later. It works fully offline and supports Apple Pencil.

Why does Rackside ask for the camera or my location?

The camera takes photos for site surveys and troubleshooting logs. Location is used only when you tap Use Current Location on a site survey, and the coordinates stay on your iPad. Both are optional, and you can change them any time in the iPad's Settings app › Rackside.

How do I export a PDF report?

Open an entry, tap the Export button at the top, then choose Export This Entry, or Export Whole Job to include every linked entry. Settings › Guide has step-by-step help.
